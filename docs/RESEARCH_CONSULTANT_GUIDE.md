## 1. What is a WorldQuant BRAIN Research Consultant?

A **Research Consultant** is a recognized quantitative researcher in WorldQuant's global crowdsourced research network. Research Consultants use the proprietary **WorldQuant BRAIN** simulation platform to discover, build, and test mathematical predictive models (**"alphas"**) that seek to forecast price movements in global financial markets.

### Key Characteristics of the Role:
* **Remote & Flexible:** Part-time, performance-driven consulting engagement allowing researchers to work from anywhere on their own schedule.
* **Access to Institutional Infrastructure:** Full access to institutional-grade financial data (fundamental, price-volume, analyst estimates, sentiment, macroeconomic), hundreds of mathematical/statistical operators, and high-performance simulation clusters.
* **Merit-Based Compensation:** Earnings are directly linked to the originality, statistical quality, and live Out-of-Sample performance of generated alphas.

## 2. Qualification & Selection Criteria

Becoming a Research Consultant is by invitation or qualification through the WorldQuant Challenge and platform milestones:

```
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│   WorldQuant User      │ ───► │  Silver / Gold Tier    │ ───► │  Research Consultant   │
│ (Simulation & Testing) │      │ (5,000 / 10,000 Points)│      │  (Contract & Payouts)  │
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

### Path to Onboarding:
1. **Accumulate Challenge Points:**
   * **Silver Tier (5,000 Points):** Initial threshold for consultant consideration and expanded operator access.
   * **Gold Tier (10,000 Points):** Standard threshold unlocking formal consultant qualification, international datasets, and high-concurrency simulation.
2. **Quality Track Record:**
   * Demonstration of consistent alpha generation across varied market sectors, asset universes, and holding horizons.
   * High In-Sample Sharpe ($> 1.25$), robust Fitness ($\ge 1.00$), controlled turnover ($1\% - 30\%$), and low self-correlation ($r < 0.70$).
3. **Formal Verification & Agreement:**
   * Passing identity verification and standard background checks.
   * Execution of the official **WorldQuant BRAIN Consulting Agreement**.

## 3. Compensation & Genius Progression Levels

The Research Consultant program features a structured progression system with increasing compensation and research privileges:

### 3.1 Compensation Model
* **Alpha Payouts:** Merit-based compensation awarded for alphas accepted into production and maintaining positive Out-of-Sample performance.
* **Activity & Performance Bonuses:** Periodic incentives, quarterly payouts, and contest rewards based on research productivity.
* **Referral Program:** Existing consultants can earn referral bonuses when introduced researchers successfully qualify and reach active consultant milestones.

### 3.2 Genius Progression Tiers
As consultants demonstrate sustained research success, they advance through formal Genius levels:

| Tier / Level | Requirements | Key Privileges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consultant (Standard)** | Qualified via Gold Tier / Challenge | Core consulting payouts, expanded concurrency, USA/Global data |
| **Master Consultant** | Proven track record of accepted production alphas | Higher compensation multipliers, priority simulation queuing |
| **Grandmaster Consultant** | Elite alpha production and multi-year consistency | Maximum payout tiers, direct quantitative researcher mentorship |

## 4. Platform Privileges & Unlocks

Upon onboarding as a Research Consultant, users unlock advanced capabilities on the BRAIN platform:

* 🌐 **Global Data Universes:** Access to international equities, including European (`EUR`), Asian (`ASI`), and Global (`GLB`) markets.
* ⚡ **High Simulation Concurrency:** Increased simultaneous simulation slots to run multi-parameter and cross-sectional scans.
* 🧬 **SuperAlpha Access:** Capability to combine multiple individual alpha signals into composite multi-factor models.
* 📊 **Institutional Risk Models:** Advanced risk attribution models (Barra-style risk factor decomposition) to test signal purity.

## 5. Career Opportunities & Institutional Hiring

The Research Consultant program acts as a primary talent incubator for WorldQuant:

* **Internships:** High-performing consultants (particularly university students and graduates) are frequently fast-tracked for Quantitative Research (QR) and Quantitative Trading (QT) internships.
* **Full-Time Opportunities:** Top consultants are regularly evaluated for full-time employment opportunities as Quantitative Researchers or Portfolio Managers within WorldQuant LLC and affiliated entities.

## 6. Compliance, Code of Conduct & Invariants

Research Consultants must strictly adhere to the following platform rules:

* 🚫 **Competition Restriction:** Once onboarded as an active Research Consultant, you may **not** participate in standard WorldQuant Challenge competitions unless an event explicitly permits consultant entry.
* 🔒 **Intellectual Property:** All submitted alphas and models are subject to the IP terms established in the WorldQuant BRAIN Consulting Agreement.
* 🛡️ **No Gaming or Noise:** Deliberate introduction of mathematical noise, curve-fitting tricks, or subverting scoring systems leads to immediate contract termination and permanent platform banning.
* 👤 **Single Account Integrity:** Only one account is permitted per consultant; sharing credentials or managing multiple accounts is strictly prohibited.
