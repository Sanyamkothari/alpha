# BRAIN Consultant Eligibility & Onboarding FAQ

**Official Reference:** [WorldQuant BRAIN Support — Can I become a BRAIN consultant? Who is eligible? (Article 4418509454999)](https://support.worldquantbrain.com/hc/en-us/articles/4418509454999-Can-I-become-a-BRAIN-consultant-Who-is-eligible)

---

## 1. Overview

The **WorldQuant BRAIN Research Consultant Program** offers qualified quants, data scientists, engineers, and students worldwide the opportunity to conduct quantitative finance research as independent consultants. Consultants are compensated based on the quality and live performance of their predictive trading models (**"alphas"**).

---

## 2. Who Is Eligible?

To be eligible to become an official Research Consultant, an applicant must satisfy the following criteria:

### 2.1 Performance Milestone
* **Gold Level Achievement:** Reach **10,000 Challenge Points** on the WorldQuant BRAIN platform by building high-performing, out-of-sample alphas.
* **Track Record of Quality:** Demonstration of consistent alpha generation with robust In-Sample Sharpe ($> 1.25$), strong Fitness ($\ge 1.00$), controlled turnover ($5\% - 30\%$), and low self-correlation ($r < 0.70$).
* *Note:* In certain global competitions (e.g., International Quant Championship - IQC) or university partnerships, accelerated invitations may be extended to top performers who reach the Silver level (5,000 pts).

### 2.2 Age & Legal Capacity
* Must be **eighteen (18) years of age or older**.
* Must have legal capacity to enter into a binding consulting contract.

### 2.3 Geographic & Residency Eligibility
* The program is open to residents of numerous countries across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and Africa.
* **Exclusions:** Individuals residing in sanctioned or prohibited jurisdictions (e.g. OFAC-sanctioned regions) are ineligible.

### 2.4 Professional & Employment Standing
* **No Conflict of Interest:** You must not be prohibited by any existing employment agreement, non-compete clause, university policy, or visa restriction from engaging in independent consulting.
* **Financial Industry Restrictions:** Full-time employees of certain competing hedge funds, proprietary trading firms, or broker-dealers may be subject to moonlighting or regulatory restrictions and must ensure compliance with their employer's policies.
* **Academic Background:** No specific degree is strictly mandated, though strong backgrounds in STEM (Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, Engineering), Data Science, or Quantitative Finance are advantageous.

## 3. The 4-Step Onboarding Process

Once an applicant achieves the required point milestone (10,000 pts / Gold Level) and receives an invitation via the BRAIN dashboard or email:

```
┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐      ┌────────────────────────┐
│ 1. Platform Invitation │ ───► │ 2. Identity & ID Check │ ───► │ 3. Background Check    │ ───► │ 4. Sign Agreement      │
│ (Reach 10K Gold Level) │      │ (Passport / Govt ID)   │      │ (Standard Screening)   │      │ (Consulting Contract)  │
└────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘      └────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Invitation & Expression of Interest:** Complete the consultant application form on the BRAIN platform dashboard.
2. **Identity Verification:** Upload government-issued photo identification (Passport, National ID, or Driver's License).
3. **Background Screening:** Complete a routine third-party background check (education/identity verification).
4. **Legal & Tax Documentation:**
   * Sign the **WorldQuant BRAIN Research Consulting Agreement**.
   * Submit appropriate tax documentation (**W-8BEN** for non-US residents or **W-9** for US persons).
   * Configure payout details (e.g., Tipalti, direct wire, or supported payment rails).

## 5. Consultant Invariants & Rules of Engagement

* **Independent Contractor Status:** Research Consultants act as independent contractors, not full-time employees. You maintain complete flexibility over your working hours and location.
* **Competition Restriction:** Once onboarded as an active Research Consultant, you may no longer participate in standard participant-level competitions (such as general WorldQuant Challenge events) unless explicitly permitted.
* **Single Account Integrity:** Only one account is permitted per person. Sharing credentials or managing multiple accounts leads to immediate contract termination and permanent banning.
* **Intellectual Property:** All submitted alphas and related research are assigned to WorldQuant under the terms of the Consulting Agreement.
